# Code of conduct

NiuPanel contributors and maintainers are expected to keep project spaces welcoming, professional, and focused on constructive technical collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Give actionable technical feedback and assume good intent.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.
- Protect private information and report security issues through the private process.
- Accept maintainer decisions made to protect users, contributors, or project sustainability.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and publication of another person's private information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's available private contact channel. Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, project chat, and public events where someone represents NiuPanel.
